FAQ

Getting Started

How Do I Make An Account?

Tap Register, pick a trainer name and a password. No email or approval needed. Your page is live straight away.

I Forgot My Password. What Do I Do?

Message an admin and they'll make you a new one. Once you're logged in, you can change it in Account Settings with Change Password.

Where Is My Page?

Tap your name at the top of the Menu. To change your picture, cover photo, name or password, tap the gear on your page to open Account Settings.

Your Personal Collection

What Is My Personal Collection?

The binders on your page that show the cards you own. They're for showing off, not for sale, so they have no prices or conditions.

How Do I Make A Binder?

On your page, tap Add Binder, or go to Account Settings › Manage Binders. Give it a name, and add a description if you like. You can have up to 50 binders.

How Do I Add Cards To My Binder?

In Manage Binders, tap Add Cards on a binder. Find your card with Search or By Set, or tap Not In The List to use your own photo. You can also add a card straight from the Scanner.

Who Can See My Binders?

Everyone can see your binders that have cards in them. Empty binders only show to you.

Scanner

What Is The Scanner?

Take a photo of a card and the Scanner tells you what it is: its set, number, versions and what it sells for. Tap the card to add it to one of your binders. You'll find it in the Menu.

How Many Scans Do I Get?

3 a day without a store and 8 a day with a store. Your scans start again at midnight. You need to be logged in to scan.

How Do I Get More Scans?

Send an admin a message. The vault team can raise your daily limit. When you run out, the Scanner shows a Message An Admin button.

The Scan Got My Card Wrong. What Now?

Check the name and number on your card and pick the right one from Other Versions. For the best read, hold the card flat, close and in good light, so the small number at the bottom is sharp. Scan one card at a time.

Ask The Vault

What Is Ask The Vault?

An AI helper. Ask it about card prices, what's in stock in the stores, or ideas for what to collect. It's free.

Is There A Limit On Ask The Vault?

You get 30 AI answers every 15 minutes. After that it keeps answering with simpler built-in answers until your time resets. The whole site also has a daily total of 900 AI answers; if that runs out, everyone gets the simpler answers until midnight.

Buying

How Do I Buy A Card?

There are two ways:

  • On the site: tap + on a card to put it in your cart, then open the cart and tap Send Order. The store has 3 days to accept it (the Big Boys Vault store has 7).
  • Outside the site: take a screenshot of the cards you want and send it to the seller, by message here or wherever you talk to them.
How Do I Pay?

Payment is between you and the seller. It can happen on the site or outside it, by GCash, Maya, bank or however you both agree. The store's payment details are on its store page. If you ordered on the site, tap I've Paid on your order once you've paid.

How Do Meetups And Shipping Work?

You and the seller agree on them directly, in your chat here or outside the site.

Is Big Boys Vault Responsible For My Purchase?

No. Big Boys Vault has no liability for any transaction, whether it takes place on the website or outside it. Check a store's vouches before you buy, and only pay people you trust.

Can I Make An Offer?

Yes, on cards from stores: tap Make An Offer on the card. The store can accept, decline or send you a counter offer. You can have one open offer per card. Cards in the Big Boys Vault store don't take offers.

Can I Cancel An Order?

Yes, while the store hasn't finished it yet. Open My Orders and tap Cancel.

How Do I Leave A Vouch?

Open the store's page and go to Vouches. Say how it went, and add photos if you like. Vouches help other trainers know who to trust.

How Do I Save A Store Or A Card For Later?

Tap Bookmark. Everything you bookmark is in the Menu under Bookmarks. To hear when a store adds new cards, tap Follow on the store.

Posts And Messages

How Do I Post?

Write in the box on the Home page or on your own page. You can add up to 4 photos, use bold and three text sizes, and add a caption to each photo. Your post shows on your page and in everyone's feed.

How Do I Message Someone?

Tap Message on their page, their store or a card. Chats can have photos and voice notes, and you can call from a chat.

Notifications

How Do I Get Alerts On My Phone?

Open Notifications and turn alerts on, then allow them when your phone asks. On an iPhone, first tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”, open Big Boys Vault from your Home Screen, and turn alerts on there.

How Do I Choose What I'm Told About?

In Notifications, each kind of alert has its own switch.

Opening A Store

How Do I Open A Store?

On your page, tap Create A Store. Tell us a bit about what you sell, add photos if you like, and send. An admin answers in your messages. Once you're approved, the button becomes Store Manager.

Is My Store Separate From My Page?

Yes. Your store has its own name, picture, cover, links and posts. Your page and personal binders stay yours. A post written on your store shows on the store, and a post written on your page shows on your page.

Adding Cards

How Do I Add Cards To My Store?

Go to Store Manager › Add Cards. Use Search, Scan A Card, By Set, or Not In The List for your own photo. For each card, pick its condition and price, and choose the online picture or your own photo.

How Many Cards Can My Store Hold?

Up to 500.

How Many Scans Do I Get As A Store?

8 a day, shared between the Scanner and Add Cards. They start again at midnight. Need more? Message an admin.

How Do Binders Work In My Store?

Binders group your cards, by set or by Pokémon for example. Go to Store Manager › Binders, or tap Edit Binders on your store. You can sort them, add a cover and description, and choose who sees them.

Orders

Someone Wants To Buy. What Do I Do?
  • An order on the site: open Store Manager › Orders and Accept or Decline within 3 days. When the money arrives, tap Mark Paid.
  • A message or a screenshot: sort out the price, payment and shipping or meetup with the buyer directly, here or outside the site.
I Sold A Card Outside The Site. What Do I Do?

Update your store straight away so buyers only see what you still have. In Store Manager › Listings, lower its stock or tap In Stock to switch it to Out Of Stock. You can also tap Edit on the card in your store and choose Out Of Stock or Hidden.

Is Big Boys Vault Responsible For My Sales?

No. Big Boys Vault has no liability for any transaction, whether it takes place on the website or outside it.

How Do I Answer An Offer?

In Orders, Accept, Decline, or Counter with your price. An accepted offer becomes an order at that price.

Your Store

I'm Busy Or Away. Can I Pause My Store?

Yes, in Store Settings. Away keeps your store open but tells buyers replies may be slow. Vacation stops new orders and offers until you're back. You can also hide your store completely.

How Do Views Work?

A view counts when someone else opens your card, once a day per person. Your own looks don't count, and neither do search engines or link previews.
